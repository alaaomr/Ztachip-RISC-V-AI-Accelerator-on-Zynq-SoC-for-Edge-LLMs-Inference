==============================================================================
LEARNING RESOURCES — ztachip ZC702 Graduation Project
Curated video lectures and reading material to understand the work done so far.
Created: 2026-05-29 for Eslam Elsayed
==============================================================================

These resources are organized by topic, ordered by priority for understanding
the work we've done on porting ztachip to Zynq ZC702 and running an LLM on it.

I'm naming the channels/series rather than dropping URLs — just search them on
YouTube or Google.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FOUNDATION — Digital design + Verilog/VHDL refresher
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why: Helps you read the RTL files we've been touching (main_zc702.v,
mem64.vhd, etc.).

  - Nandland (Russell Merrick)
      "Verilog Tutorial" playlist. Plain English, breadboard-style
      explanations of registers, FSMs, FIFOs.

  - VLSI Academy
      VHDL fundamentals if you prefer VHDL.

  - ZipCPU blog by Dan Gisselquist
      Text + occasional videos. Best free resource on writing real RTL.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ZYNQ + VIVADO + BLOCK DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why: Directly maps to what we did with zynq_ps_bd.tcl and
create_project_zc702.tcl.

  - Adam Taylor's "MicroZed Chronicles"
      Hundreds of short Zynq articles + videos. He covers PS7 configuration,
      FCLK, DDR, MIO mapping — exactly the stuff in our BD script.

  - Digilent FPGA tutorials
      They make Arty/Zedboard. Their "Getting Started with Vivado IP
      Integrator" series shows block design clicks step by step.

  - Xilinx (AMD) official YouTube channel
      Search for "Vivado Quick Take" playlists. Short, focused, authoritative.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. AXI PROTOCOL — critical (we used HP0 AXI3 for DMA)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why: Understand this and S_AXI_HP0 in our design stops being a mystery.

  - "AXI Basics" by Mohammad Tahghighi
      Clear YouTube playlist covering AXI4-Lite, AXI4-Full, and AXI3
      differences (which matters for us!).

  - Sandeep Lal's "Mastering AXI Protocol" series
      Deeper dive.

  - Xilinx WP 1037 "AXI Reference Guide"
      PDF, not video, but the bible for protocol details.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. RISC-V SOFT CORES (VexRiscv is one of these)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Onur Mutlu's "Digital Design and Computer Architecture" course at
    ETH Zurich
      Entire course free on YouTube. The single best CS architecture course
      I'd recommend.

  - CNLohr YouTube channel
      Search "RISC-V on FPGA". Fun hands-on series synthesizing tiny RISC-V
      cores.

  - VexRiscv's own GitHub README
      Architecture diagrams worth reading (not video, but short).


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SIMULATION, TESTBENCHES & STATIC TIMING ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why: Explains what we saw in xsim and why our WNS = -1.449 ns mattered.

  - Sunburst Design (Cliff Cummings) "FSM coding" lectures
      Classic, available on YouTube.

  - VLSI Pro / VLSI Concepts
      Great "STA Made Easy" playlist explaining setup/hold, WNS, WHS in
      beginner terms.

  - Xilinx YouTube "Vivado Timing Closure" series
      Practical Vivado-specific tips.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. TRANSFORMERS / LLM ARCHITECTURE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why: This is what the 8 LLM kernels we verified are actually doing inside
a model (sine, cosine, RoPE, RMS, softmax, etc.)

  - Andrej Karpathy: "Let's build GPT: from scratch, in code, spelled out"
      2-hour YouTube video. THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT RESOURCE ON THIS LIST.
      After watching it you'll understand exactly why test_llm_rms,
      test_llm_softmax, test_llm_rope matter.

  - 3Blue1Brown's "Neural Networks" playlist
      Especially Chapter 5 "But what is a GPT?" and Chapter 6 "Attention".
      Beautifully animated.

  - Jay Alammar's blog "The Illustrated Transformer"
      Not video, but the most-recommended visual explainer ever.

  - MIT 6.S191 "Introduction to Deep Learning"
      Free YouTube course, updated yearly. Has a transformer lecture.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. QUANTIZATION (for Level 4 — converting SmolLM2 to ZUF)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - "Hugging Face NLP Course" Chapter 8
      Covers GGUF and quantization basics.

  - Maarten Grootendorst "Visual Guide to Quantization"
      Search YouTube. Excellent.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. FPGA-BASED AI ACCELERATORS — the big picture
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why: Understand WHY we're doing this — context for your graduation defense.

  - Berkeley CS294 "Hardware for Machine Learning"
      Free lectures on YouTube. Covers DSAs (Domain-Specific Architectures)
      like ztachip.

  - Stanford CS217 "Hardware Accelerators for Machine Learning"
      Kunle Olukotun, free YouTube.

  - Hot Chips / MICRO conference talks on YouTube
      Search "Hot Chips ML accelerator". Industry talks on Google TPU,
      NVIDIA, Cerebras. Inspirational.
